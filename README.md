## 🚀 About Me

<div align="justify">

I am an **AI Researcher and MPhil researcher in Civil Engineering at Central South University, China**, with an academic foundation in **Computer Science** and research experience spanning **computer vision, 3D perception, multimodal learning, structural health monitoring, railway infrastructure inspection, digital twins, physics-informed machine learning, and Medical AI**.

My current work focuses on developing intelligent systems that can transform heterogeneous infrastructure observations — including **images, point clouds, monocular depth, sensor measurements, and time-series signals** — into reliable information for **condition assessment, structural monitoring, predictive maintenance, and infrastructure decision support**.

A central theme of my research is the integration of **data-driven AI with physical knowledge and engineering constraints**, particularly for real-world environments where observations may be sparse, noisy, incomplete, or affected by poor visibility and domain shift.

Before moving into intelligent infrastructure research, I worked on applied AI and medical computer vision at the **National Center of Artificial Intelligence (NCAI), Pakistan**, and **NeuroCare.AI, USA**, contributing to projects involving MRI, CT, X-ray, mammography, ultrasound, and AI-assisted clinical decision support.

## 🌐 Current Research Direction

<div align="justify">

My long-term research goal is to develop **predictive infrastructure intelligence systems** that move beyond isolated defect detection toward continuous understanding of infrastructure condition and evolution.

I am particularly interested in frameworks that integrate:

**multimodal sensing → robust representation learning → physics-guided state estimation → uncertainty modeling → temporal degradation tracking → prediction → risk-aware maintenance decisions**

with applications in **railways, transportation infrastructure, structural systems, and autonomous infrastructure inspection**.
