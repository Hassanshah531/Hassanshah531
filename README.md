## 🚀 About Me

<div align="justify">

I am an **AI Researcher and MPhil researcher in Civil Engineering at Central South University, China**, with a previous degree in **Computer Science**.

My work lies at the intersection of **computer vision and engineering**, where I develop learning-based systems for railway inspection, structural condition assessment, 3D scene understanding, predictive monitoring, and physics-informed modeling.

My earlier research experience includes **Medical AI**, where I worked on deep-learning systems for brain imaging, tuberculosis screening, and breast-cancer analysis at the National Center of Artificial Intelligence, Pakistan, and NeuroCare.AI, USA.

I am particularly interested in building AI systems that move beyond isolated prediction toward reliable understanding of real-world physical systems.
